## The Story that Launched a Thousand Covers
This cultural project is centered on the changes in covers throughout time for some of the most
well known stories: the Iliad and Odyssey

This is just the launch page. All of my work on my individual dataset will be in the 'Custom Dataset' directory and all my information of the dataset I'm auditing will be under the 'Dataset audit' folder. Each will have their own reflection page and the Dataset audit will have my plans to connect the two.

Fun fact about the quote I'm tweaking for my project title! The original quote "the face that lauched a thousand ships" (referring to Helen) is in fact not from the Iliad or the Odyssey but actually from Christopher Marlowe's *Dr. Faustus*
