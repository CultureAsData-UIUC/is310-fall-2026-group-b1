It will be helpful to define exact parameters for these categories before you move forward. Adding columns to an existing file could be part of the workflow, but I would like to understand what source research and interpretation you will contribute. Is one row a tale, a particular version, or a claim about its origins? You could create records that preserve the collector, language, publication context, attribution, and evidence for how a selected version has been described. The dataset you linked to seems to be just the full text of each story with the name. Do you plan to use the text of the stories themselves? Since you already have the stories collected here you could consider using the text of the stories themselves to add to the cultural complexity of the question you are asking. You could also consider adding some German  folktales that are not Grimm' fairy tales, possibly as a point of comparison.
