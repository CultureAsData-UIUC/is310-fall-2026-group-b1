### Aurora - Arthuriana in film

My focus is on Arthurian legends. Arthurian legends refers to stories surrounding the mythos of King Arthur. This includes medieval literature dating back nearly 1000 years, which will be the focus of my database audit: https://www.arthurianfiction.org/. It also includes post-medieval stories continuing the Arthurian tradition. The database I create will be focused particularly on how the long history of Arthurian legend is adapted on screen in the form of movies and television. In The Use of Arthurian Legend in Hollywood Film, authors Samuel L. Umland and Rebecca A. Umland state that “the films that employ features of [Arthurian] legend are a heterogenous collection: They have very different purposes and use the legend–or, more accurately, activate only certain features of it–to achieve their particular ends” (p. 4). This is very different from the earlier legends which are dictated by a “tyranny of tradition” to maintain largely the same “generic forms and conventions” (p. 4). My key interest in both datasets is how stories from different times and creators build off of one another. For example, what prior Arthurian stories (including other films) are specific story elements (plot, characters, characterization, character relationships, themes, etc.) in Monty Python and the Holy Grail based upon? This directly connects to our group theme of retellings of old folklore and legend as I will be exploring how the legends of King Arthur developed in the medieval period (largely through many retellings of the same basic plots) and how these legends are retold and changed on screen.

Umland, R. A., & Umland S. J. (1996). The use of Arthurian legend in Hollywood film: From Connecticut Yankees to fisher kings. Greenwood Press.
