Initial dataset: supernatural encounters in Andrew Lang’s Arabian Nights
60 manually coded narrative-event observations from the 1898 English adaptation, using the Gutenberg HTML text. Not 60 different stories or cross-cultural examples. Rows are narrative events nested in three story sections; the first 55 events are from the merchant and first old man story. The second old man section contributes five.

Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/files/128/old/128-h/128-h.htm

Unit: one event from one documented edition. jinn_explicit is yes only when the acting character is the genius, not for magical humans. supernatural_role is a researcher coding for analysis; cultural_meaning is intentionally blank because no sourced interpretation has been established. The counts by motif and role are descriptive of this chosen excerpt, not generalizable to Arabic or South Asian folklore.

Important: line references are to the web-extracted Gutenberg HTML used during collection, and should be spot-checked against the text before submission. To fulfill a genuinely cross-cultural comparison, add separately sourced story versions from another documented tradition.
