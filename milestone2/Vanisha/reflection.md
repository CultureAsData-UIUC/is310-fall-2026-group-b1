# Milestone 2 reflection

At first, I wanted to compare supernatual stories across many cultures, but the feedback helped me realize that I needed a smaller and more specific starting point. I chose one documented version of The Arabian Nights and started by recording individual events. This made the work easier to organize and gave me something I could actually analyze with Python.

One thing that is working is the structure of the CSV. I can see which events involve supernatural beings, which ones are about threats or help, and how often different labels appear. The script also helps me catch missing values and duplicate IDs. I like that I can go back from each row to the original source.

The biggest problem is that 60 events do not mean 60 different stories. Most of the rows come from just one section, and many describe ordinary actions instead of jinn directly. My motif labels are also too specific in some places, which makes comparisons harder. Another issue is that I am using an English adaptation, so I cannot assume it represents the meaning of a story in every culture where similar stories appear.

My next step is to double-check the passage references, simplify the motif categories, and find a second clearly documented collection to compare. I also want to keep the difference between what I think a story means and what a scholar or original source says it means. I would appreciate feedback on whether my unit of analysis should stay as individual events or change to one row per motif appearance in a story version.
